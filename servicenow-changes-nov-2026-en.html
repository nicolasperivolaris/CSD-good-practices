CHANGES - ServiceNow Nov 26

Overview changes in ServiceNow as from November 2026

No more P5

We will only have P1 to P4 incidents.

All P5 incidents will become P4 incidents.

Priorities will be automatic based on the Urgency and impact.

LevelDescriptionDefinition

Defence wide

VIP

Operations & Exercises

Impacts a service used/available for all members of Belgian Defence (e.g. VPN, SharePoint, mail, Teams, HRM@Defence…).

People within Defence that need immediate assistance:

  • VIPs
  • Operations – Personnel in Operations
  • Exercises – Personnel in exercises

2

MODERATE

Barrack

Specialised Service

All users from a barrack or building within a barrack cannot access one or multiple services.

A specialized service is a service not used by everyone within the Belgian Defence (e.g. Total Health, ServiceNow, Jira, Alice, …)

3

LOW

User level

User(s) of a service is impacted, but other users of the same service are not impacted.

Incident Urgency

LevelDefinitionTypical Characteristics

1

HIGH

The issue needs to be resolved immediately because the business impact will increase rapidly if delayed.

  • Critical time sensitivity
  • Work stoppage risk is imminent
  • Deadlines are at risk

2

MODERATE

The issue needs resolution soon, but not immediately; impact will increase over time.

  • Moderate time sensitivity
  • Some business impact, but workaround may exist
  • Delay causes inconvenience or reduced efficiency

3

LOW

The issue can be resolved at a convenient time with minimal business impact.

  • Little or no time pressure
  • No significant business risk
  • Often cosmetic or minor issues

IMPACT + URGENCY = PRIORITY

P1 = call Service provider!

Mandatory fields & audit trail

More fields will be mandatory, for example:

  • Description
  • Assigned to (when ticket goes to “in progress”)

If fields in the Incident ticket are adapted this will be reflected in the audit trail with the name of the person responsible for the adaptation, like for example:

  • When changing (Short) Description
  • When changing Urgency and/or Impact

Self-service via portal

Finaly there will also be the possibility for ITIL users to create an Incident Ticket through the service portal, this is a test. These tickets need to be completed by CSD based on the info provided. Please provide feedback concerning these tickets. We are mainly interested in the completeness of the info provided, the workload to complete the ticket and possible improvement proposals for this way of introducing a ticket. The number of tickets introduced through the service portal should be very limited as this functionality is only available for ITIL users and the availability of this option will only be communicated to ITIL users from YYYYY.
